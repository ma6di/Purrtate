Purrtate

Keep cat toys feeling new.

Purrtate splits your cat’s toys into three baskets. One is out, two rest. It tells you when to swap, checks each basket has a good mix, and suggests when a new toy is worth getting.

No account, no tracking. Everything stays on your iPhone.
