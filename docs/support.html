Common questions

How do I swap baskets?
On Today, tap Swap to Basket … and confirm. You can swap early if your cat is already bored of the toys.

How often should I swap?
The suggestion depends on your cat’s energy level, but it’s only a starting point. Change it in Settings → Swap baskets every. If your cat ignores the toys before swap day, pick fewer days.

I’m not getting reminders.
Turn on Settings → Swap reminder in the app. If iOS says notifications are off, enable them in iOS Settings → Notifications → Purrtate.

What does the “!” on a basket mean?
The basket has fewer than 2 toys, or is missing a chase, stalk or grab toy. Tap it to see what to add or move.

How do I start over?
Settings → Erase all data. This can’t be undone.

Is my data backed up?
Only through your normal iPhone backups. The app has no account or cloud of its own.
