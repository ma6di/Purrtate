Privacy policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Short version: Purrtate collects no personal data. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

What the app stores

Your cat’s name and energy level, the names of your baskets and toys, their type and colour, and when baskets were swapped. This is saved only in the app’s storage on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Purrtate has no server and makes no network requests. We, the developer, never see or receive your data.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules a local reminder on your iPhone for the day a basket swap is due. It’s created on the device by iOS and isn’t sent through any server. You can turn it off in the app’s Settings or in iOS Settings.

Backups

If you use iCloud Backup or back up to a computer, iOS may include the app’s data in that backup, as it does for most apps. Those backups are handled by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy.

Third parties

None. The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs, and shares no data with anyone.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Erase all data in the app, or delete the app. Either removes everything it stored.

Children

Purrtate collects no data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you have a question, write to mahdi.cheraghali+purrtate@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
